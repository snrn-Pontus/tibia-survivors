Progression rework · pitch 3

Play Tibia's Endgame in 30 Minutes

Tibia already has the best build system any survivors game could want. It's just spread over two years of grinding: the Wheel of Destiny, imbuement slots on real gear, the Exaltation Forge, the bestiary's charms, prey. I want to compress it into one run, keep the real numbers, and let Tibia's own limits make the choices.

4Tibia endgame systems, each with one job
0invented perks left in the level-up deck
176items in our loot data with real imbuement slots
36Wheel slots per vocation, straight from io_wheel.cpp

"The game's identity is real Tibia rules, yet its progression is the most invented part. I'd flip that."

Ana Lucía "Lu" Ferreira · systems designer · playing since 7.4, running Canary servers since it was OTX
II · The problem

What's wrong with the level-up today

Combat, loot, monsters, formulas: all Canary. Then you level up, and you're in a different game. These numbers come from SKILLS.md, character.ts and the README's own "Not real" list.

6

Imbuement slots tied to nothing

21 imbuements arrive as cards and fill MAX_SLOTS = 6. A sorcerer holding a wand of inferno (no imbuement slot in Tibia) can carry Powerful Vampirism 25%, Void 8%, Strike +40%, Epiphany +4, Swiftness +30 and Dragon Hide 15%, all at once.

22

Charms on every creature

All 22 playable charms work on everything. Each creature's 5 / 25 / 100 kills in a run offers 3 charm cards: Rookgaard Fields alone (5 creatures) can open 15 charm screens. Low Blow III costs 4,000 charm points in Tibia; here it's one Epic card (7%).

4→3

Four systems, three stats

Crit comes from Strike, Low Blow, Savage Blow, spell grades and Blood Rage. Leech from Vampirism, Void, Vampiric Embrace, Void's Call, grades and Blood Rage. Every system feeds crit, leech or element: picking is accounting.

1%

Legendary that can't exist

Rarity multiplies x1 / x2 / x3 / x5. But imbuements, charms and spell grades have 3 tiers, so a Legendary roll on 3 of the 5 headline systems silently drops to Epic (fitRarity). Rarity means "more", never "different".

24

The Wheel as a lookup table

Only 24 spells get Wheel grades; the rest get an invented +5 / 10 / 15%. No points, no domains, no revelation perks, no avatars. The system that has defined Tibia's vocations since 2023 is a damage table.

8

Invented evolutions

"Avatar of Steel" here is a flat 30% damage cut from Shield Training 10 + a protection imbuement. The real Avatar of Steel is a 15-second, 100%-crit revelation spell. Same name, different thing, and players notice.

And loot is a stat stick. A dragon slayer (3 imbuement slots in Tibia) and a giant sword differ only by attack in Character.score(). The thing Tibia players care most about when they see a drop (slots, classification, tier) doesn't exist yet. Meanwhile skill advances add a card every 20 s (Game.advance), so at x50 training the deck is mostly filler.

III · The vision

One run, Tibia's whole endgame, one job per system

Today four systems compete for the same three stats from the same deck. In Tibia they don't overlap, because each lives somewhere different: on your level, on your gear, on a creature, on your account. I copy that split.

Wheel of Destiny

the level-up spine · real
Every level gives promotion points for your vocation's wheel: HP, mana, mitigation, convictions, spell augments, and revelation perks at 3 thresholds.
Limit: 800 points; a run reaches 200-300. You commit to 1-2 of 4 domains.

Imbuements on gear

what you wear · real
Each item has its real slot count (1-3) and allowed types. Loot and the shop decide what you can imbue.
Limit: 9 slots at best (weapon 3, armor 2, helmet 2, shield 1, boots 1), only with the right items.

Exaltation Forge

duplicates become procs · real
Fuse two identical items to raise the tier: Onslaught, Ruse, Momentum, Transcendence, Amplification.
Limit: the item's real classification caps the tier (1, 2, 3 or 10).

Charms

the bestiary's reward · real
Completing bestiary entries across runs earns charm points; charms are bought and assigned to one creature each.
Limit: 2 assigned charms (6 with an unlock). Targeted, not global.

Prey

this zone's creatures · real
Unchanged mechanics (Canary's ioprey.cpp), now the "hunt" card on level-ups; prey wildcards from bosses.
Limit: 3 prey slots.

Boosts

the meta lever · not real, on purpose
XP, Loot, Gold, Haste, training, Luck: unchanged. Loot Boost now also feeds the Forge with duplicates.
Limit: level 10 each, as now.

The Wheel of Destiny becomes the level-up

The game already reads io_wheel.cpp for spell grades. I want the rest of it: the 36 slots, the four domains, dedication per point, convictions on full slots, revelation perks, gem vessels.

How it plays

  • 4 promotion points per level from 8, placed through the level-up cards (two reachable slots + a hunt card), or freely in the Wheel window while paused (Tibia lets you respec in a protection zone; the pause menu is ours).
  • Fill order is Tibia's: inner ring first (50 → 75 → 100 → 150 → 200, getSlotPrioritaryOrder), so reaching a domain's big 200 slot is a path, not a click.
  • Today's spell grades become a consequence. Each augmented spell sits in two slots (Front Sweep: Red 200 and Blue 50), matching the two Wheel grades the game already extracts; the third stays the supreme gem mod. No grade card needed.
  • Revelation at 50 / 100 / 200 domain points (real 250 / 500 / 1,000, scaled ÷5): +4 / +9 / +20 flat damage and healing, plus the domain's perk at stage I / II / III.
  • Gems from boss chests, one per domain: Lesser, Regular, Greater, with real basic and supreme mods (wheel_gems.cpp). Cooldown gems add Momentum, which is how Tibia does "free casts".

  • Classification caps the tier, from item_tiers.lua: class 1 → T1, 2 → T2, 3 → T3, 4 → T10. Our loot has 130 class-1, 152 class-2, 23 class-3 and 18 class-4 items (chain bolter L60, ravenwing L65, ghazbaran oyoroi L75, the umbral masters). That cap is the late game: chasing a class-4 piece and feeding it duplicates.
  • Mages are different, as in Tibia. Every wand and rod is class 1 (T1 at most), so a sorcerer's Forge is the helmet (Momentum), legs (Transcendence) and boots (Amplification). A paladin's is the chain bolter. Identity for free.

The real proc chances are small, and I'm keeping them. Survivors density does the work: a druid's wave hits 8 creatures, each rolling Onslaught; a crowd lands several hits a second, each rolling Ruse. Momentum at 4% every 2 s fires about every 50 s, and it's the one players will feel first. If the sim says it's too subtle, there's one knob (FORGE_SCALE), and it's the first thing I'd look at.
VII · System 4

Charms become what they are: the bestiary's reward

In Tibia you finish a bestiary entry, earn charm points, buy a charm, and put it on one creature. That's targeted power you earn by hunting, and it's the perfect meta layer for a roguelite.

  • Earn: when a creature's kills over all your runs (the save already counts them) reach its real toKill ÷10, its real CharmsPoints pay out once: rat 25 kills → 5 points; dragon lord 100 kills → 25; behemoth 250 → 50.
  • Buy between runs on a charm board, at real costs ÷5 (Low Blow 160 / 240 / 800). Buying a major tier gives minor charm echoes (50 / 100 / 200, from iobestiary.cpp) for the minor charms, as in Tibia.
  • Assign each charm to one creature: 2 slots, 6 with the "Charm Expansion" unlock (Tibia: free 2, premium 6). Reassign free between runs, for gold in a run.

Why ÷5 on costs: our 139 creatures with a bestiary entry hold 3,215 charm points in all; Canary ships 844 non-boss creatures, about 5x more. Dividing costs by 5 keeps Tibia's proportion of "how much of the board a full bestiary buys". It's the only scaled constant in the charm system. The bestiary window's info reveals use the real FirstUnlock / SecondUnlock ÷10 instead of today's fixed 5 / 25 / 100.

Day-one gift: the save migration (version 3 → 4) pays charm points for every entry an existing player has already completed, from Save.kills. Returning players open the game to a full charm board.
VIII · Kept, sharpened

Prey and boosts

Prey stays (it's already real)

Damage 2s+5%, defense 2s+10%, experience and loot 3s+10% (ioprey.cpp), 3 slots, creatures of the current zone. It becomes the level-up's hunt card, next to two Wheel cards. New: prey wildcards (real) from boss chests reroll a prey's bonus type. Prey's XP and loot bonuses take over the job of today's invented run bonuses (+20% per pick), which retire.

Boosts stay the meta lever

XP, Loot, Gold, Haste, training: x2 → x50, unchanged. They're the one deliberate non-Tibia lever and the README says so. Luck changes meaning: no multipliers left to scale, so it moves quality: the 4th card, prey star bands, and gem quality from boss chests. Loot Boost gains a second job: duplicates for the Forge.

Retired: run bonuses, Luck cards, the 8 evolutions (Draken Boots survives as the real item: draken boots, L80, class 2, 1 slot, already in our loot), and skill-advance cards (at x50 training they add a card every 20 s; levels carry the cadence now). Boss chests drop real endgame things instead: a promotion scroll, a gem, exalted cores, sometimes an imbuement scroll or a prey wildcard, and the 35% blessing stays.

On caps: Tibia's caps are the design

9 imbuement slots at best; tiers capped by classification; 800 Wheel points of which a run reaches 200-300; 2 or 6 charms; 3 prey; one gem per domain. Every cap forces a choice, which is exactly what today's "6 slots, then only upgrades" tries to fake. Late-game depth (past level ~70 in an endless run): your second and third domains, Greater gems and their supreme mods, class-4 gear climbing toward tier 10 (Onslaught 9.05%, Momentum 24.05%, Amplification +57.4%) on Loot-Boost duplicates, and the charm board growing across runs. Plenty of depth, and none of it invented.

XI · The README

What moves from "Not real" to "real"

Today's "Not real" line

  • imbuements as level-up rewards
  • charms working on every creature (in Tibia you assign each to one)
  • card rarity
  • spells without Wheel data getting a +5/10/15% grade
  • the boosts, luck (stays: the deliberate lever)
  • quick-looting, the shop in the pause menu, selling anywhere (stay)
  • diagonal steps, spears coming back, fleeing at the screen edge, spawning around you (stay)

New "What's real" rows

  • Wheel of Destiny: slots, dedication, convictions, revelation perks, gems (io_wheel.cpp, player_wheel.cpp, wheel_gems.cpp)
  • Imbuements: per-item slots and allowed types, tiers, materials (items.xml, imbuements.xml)
  • Exaltation Forge: classification caps, tier formulas, Onslaught / Ruse / Momentum / Transcendence / Amplification (item_tiers.lua, item.cpp, config.lua.dist)
  • Bestiary and charms: entry kill counts, charm points, costs, echoes, one creature per charm (monster files, bestiary_charms.lua, iobestiary.cpp)

New, honest "Not real" entries

  • The compression: Wheel ÷5 at 4 points per level, bestiary kills ÷10, charm costs ÷5, forge gold ÷100, imbuing gold ÷10, materials ÷5, avatar and Gift of Life cooldowns in minutes
  • The shrine selling materials no creature here drops; fusions never losing tier; imbuements lasting the run

XIII · Risks

Risks, honestly

Complexity
Four systems is a lot for a game where you only steer. The Wheel alone has 36 slots.
Each system shows up where it lives: the Wheel on level-ups as 2 cards (the planner is optional), imbuing and forge in pause tabs, charms between runs. The bot plays it all: an "auto" Wheel path per vocation and auto-imbue, the same code the sim uses.
Data extraction
Wheel slot effects are C++ functions, not data. About 17 revelation and augment spells aren't in data.json.
Transcribe io_wheel.cpp once (36 × 4 rows) with a hash check that warns when the Canary pin moves. Item slots, classification, bestiary, imbuements and forge are plain data in files we already fetch (items.xml, appearances.dat, monster files).
Item instances
Equipment is item ids today; imbuements and tiers need per-item state. About 40 call sites.
Mechanical refactor, phase 1, before anything else. Character.imbued() keeps its signature and reads worn gear, so every combat caller stays untouched.
Pacing
Real values are tuned for months. Early runs could feel bare (no Vampirism card at level 15); the Forge could be too subtle.
Every compression is one named constant (Wheel scale, points per level, kill ÷10, cost ÷5, gold ÷10 / ÷100, influenced rate), tuned against the sim's new columns. Gates: first imbue by ~L20, revelation I by ~L33, ±15% damage vs baseline at L30 / 60 / 100.
Dedication weight
Wheel HP at x5 per point is far heavier against a level-100 health pool than in Tibia.
DEDICATION_SCALE between 2 and 5, set by the sim; convictions and revelations stay at real values.

XV · The ask

Let me build phase 0 and 1

Two weeks: the data extraction and imbuements on real gear. It's the smallest slice that proves the idea: every drop becomes interesting, one "Not real" line disappears, and the item-instance refactor everything else needs gets done. If the sim and your own runs don't feel better after that, we stop there, and the game still has a cleaner gear system.

The game already took Tibia's monsters, spells, loot and formulas. Let's take its endgame too.
