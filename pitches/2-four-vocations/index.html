A level-up rework for Tibia Survivors

Four Vocations, Four Games

KnightPaladinSorcererDruid

Right now, picking a vocation changes your sprite, your spell list and which potion you drink. It doesn't change how you level up. I want each vocation to be its own game, built from Tibia's own Wheel of Destiny, so that a sorcerer's level 30 and a knight's level 30 are two different things.

1. What's wrong today

The systems are good, and they're real Tibia. The trouble is that they all point at the same few stats, so every vocation's run converges on one build.

6 of 8candidate slots per level-up are vocation-blind: 2 imbuements, prey, 2 run bonuses, luck
13 / 20of the sorcerer's gradable spells grade as the same flat +5/10/15%. For the druid it's 12 of 18
3 systemssell life leech (Vampirism 25%, Vampiric Embrace 3.2%, Front Sweep / Terra Wave grades 5%), and 3 sell crit
1% → 0A Legendary roll can never land on an imbuement, charm or grade. The rarest roll in the game buys gold

What this means

1. Most of a run happens before a vocation's spells show up. A typical sorcerer spends its whole hour as a level 8–15 character with Energy Strike and a wand, so the only "sorcerer" cards it can see are "Energy Strike +5%". Its identity has to come from the level-up cards, starting at level 9. That's why element cards also work on wand and rod hits of their element: a wand of vortex feeds Storm Caller, a snakebite rod feeds Earthshaper.

2. Our own bot doesn't choose the build. The sim's pick order (src/sim.ts) is run bonus Experience, then training, then loot and gold, and imbuements and grades only after that. If the "sensible" pick is always the economy, the build is decoration.

2. The vision

Each vocation gets its own card pool, made of three paths. Each path has a playstyle, a keystone that changes how you fight, and a Wheel of Destiny revelation as its peak.

Three rules

  1. Cards change how you play, not just your numbers. "Spells recast" and "hits strike twice" instead of "+5% damage".
  2. Two cards for your vocation, one universal. Leech, crit, prey and luck still exist, in a small shared pool.
  3. Real Tibia where it exists. Wheel revelations (Avatar of Storm, Twin Bursts, Executioner's Throw, Divine Grenade), Forge tiers (Momentum, Onslaught), Tibia conditions (burning, poisoned, electrified). Invented cards are labelled and go into README's "Not real".

What you get for it

  • 12 builds instead of 1, and a reason to play the other three characters.
  • Spell grades get a home: grading Energy Beam is a step down the Storm Caller path.
  • Bosses become goals: their chests hand out revelations.
  • Endless runs get a late game: uncapped Mastery cards once a path is complete.
  • A sorcerer feels like a sorcerer from level 9, with its wand.

Part of: Mastermind

The roll

  • 2 vocation cards: path nodes and the grades of your spells. Paths you've invested in weigh x2, and a path with a pending keystone x3.
  • 1 universal card: imbuement, protection, prey or luck.
  • A 4th card with luck, as today (luck / 2 %, at most 50%).
  • Fillers (training, potions, gold) only when nothing else fits, and Mastery replaces them once a path is maxed.

4. Paths, keystones, revelations

Every path has the same shape: 3 minor nodes (3 ranks each), 2 notables (2 ranks, Rare or rarer), a keystone (Epic, from 5 path points) and a revelation (Legendary, from 12 points plus the keystone, one per run). Wheel grades of the path's spells count as points too. You can mix two paths, but you get at most two keystones and one revelation.

Fire, energy and death magic, and runes. Today: a stronger wand. Proposed: three ways to break the spellbook.

Storm Caller

Energy Strike, Energy Beam, Great Energy Beam, Energy Wave, Rage of the Skies

Death Strike, Flame Strikes, Fire Wave, Hell's Core, Great Death Beam

12. What rarity means now

Today rarity is a multiplier, and three-tier cards cut it off, so Legendary means nothing on the cards that matter. In my system rarity decides what kind of card it is. The odds and luck stay exactly as they are (rarity.ts).

RarityOdds (luck 0 → 50)Path cardUniversal card
Common70% → 50%+1 rank of a minor node, or +1 grade+1 tier / x1, as today
Rare22% → 31.4%+2 ranks, a notable's rank I, or +2 grades+2 tiers / x2
Epic7% → 15%+3 ranks, a notable maxed, or a keystone (half of Epic rolls once you have 5 points)+3 tiers / x3
Legendary1% → 3.6%A revelation (12 points + keystone)x5 on uncapped cards, and Gift of Life

Never stuck on luck

Once you have 5 points (and know a spell the keystone needs), the keystone is guaranteed within 3 level-ups. At 12 points, the next boss chest offers your revelation against two Epic cards. Today 60% of chests give a single pick. Bosses become something to plan a run around.

Legendary is an event

Avatar of Storm, Twin Bursts' big brother, Executioner's Throw, Divine Grenade: real Wheel revelations, one per run. When the orange frame shows up, the run changes.

13. Hands-off players get it too

Combat is fully automatic (src/autocast.ts), so every card has to pay off for someone who only steers.

  • Procs need nothing. Free casts, echoes, twin strikes, cleave, ricochet, burns, poison and blocks all live inside Game.cast and weaponHit.
  • Spell choice follows the build. expected() gets multiplied by the path's value (echo chance, chain targets, Runic Mastery, Mastery stacks), so a Storm Caller casts beams. The 0.9 rune penalty rises with Thrifty Charges, so a Rune Master leans on runes.
  • Stance thresholds move into path data: Battle Instinct starts Blood Rage with 1 adjacent creature (instead of 2), Juggernaut starts Protector at 60% health (instead of 45%), and Grovekeeper heals at 80% (instead of 60%) and casts heals as attacks when creatures are near.
  • Avatars and auto spells fire on their own when something is within 4 tiles.
  • Movement cards are only 4 of 84, and chase mode counts as moving. Standing players have Holy Avenger and Grovekeeper's Deep Roots.
  • The level-up screen stars the card from your main path.

14. Why this, not that

AlternativeWhat it gets rightWhy paths beat it
A deeper shared pool: more stats, more cardsCheap, no per-vocation workIt makes the convergence worse. Four characters still play one game, with more ways to type "+5%"
A between-runs talent wheel (meta tree)Long-term goalsThe fun happens in the run, at level-ups. Path Attunement and the Codex give meta goals on top of paths anyway
More Vampire Survivors evolutionsBig momentsEvolutions are rare (boss chests) and mostly economy. Paths make every level-up a build decision, and revelations are evolution-sized moments that fit the class
Rebalancing numbers and rarity onlySmallest changeIt fixes Legendary but not the core problem: the cards don't care what vocation you are
The one-line case: we already have four vocations with real Tibia spells and the Wheel of Destiny's class data in the repo. Paths turn that data into four games, so content we already have becomes replay value.

15. Risks, honestly

Risks

  • Card count: 84 path cards to balance.
  • Proc chains (echo → arc → poison → contagion) can snowball or cost frame time.
  • Invented mechanics dilute "real Tibia inside a run".
  • Slower leveling without XP run bonuses on level-ups.
  • Hands-off players miss movement cards.
  • Four vocations = four times the bugs.

Mitigations

  • One vocation per phase, each gated on the sim (±15% between paths).
  • Procs never re-proc (from.src), conditions only on primary hits, Contagion max 3, one pending echo per spell. All node effects compile into one Mods object once per pick, so there's no per-hit lookup.
  • Every card carries its origin, and invented ones go into README's "Not real". Values are built on real ones (Onslaught's 60%, Canary's stances, Tibia's condition ticks).
  • Run bonuses move to boss chests, boosts are untouched, and the sim compares level at 60:00.
  • 4 of 84 cards are movement cards, chase mode counts, and the Recommended star helps.
  • A PATHS flag keeps today's system live until each vocation passes.

Give me 11 days: the framework and the Sorcerer.
