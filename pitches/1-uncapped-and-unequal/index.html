Tibia Survivors · level-up and skill system rework

Uncapped & Unequal

Every pick keeps stacking, so a run never runs out of good cards. A rarer card isn't just more of the same: it's a different card.

Pitched by Mira Halvorsen, roguelite designer (survivors-likes, Brotato-style shops, Hades boons, PoE affixes)
0 hard tier caps 4 rarities that each add a different thing 1 curve for every stat 24 rule-breaking relics, 3 per run Tibia's real tiers kept as ranks 1 to 3

1The hook

Play a sorcerer for an hour in the headless sim and look at the character at the end. Level 43. All six imbuements at Powerful. Every spell grade maxed: 13 out of 13. Twenty of the 22 bestiary charms at tier III. All four run bonuses at 5/5, luck at 50/50.

That character has nothing left to want. Every level-up after that is gold, potions, an exercise wand or yet another prey re-roll. The game's most exciting moment, the card screen, has turned into a chore.

"In a survivors game the level-up screen is the slot machine. Ours pays out three cherries for 40 minutes, then only coins."

My fix is two moves that work together:

Uncapped
Basic, Intricate and Powerful stay exactly as Tibia has them. They become ranks 1 to 3 of a line that keeps going: Powerful +1, +2, +3... Each extra rank adds less than the one before, but never zero. No card ever drops out of the pool because it's full.
Unequal
Every card gives one rank. Rarity adds a different kind of bonus on top: Rare adds a secondary stat, Epic adds a proc, Legendary adds a relic, a rule-breaker like "your critical hits chain to a second creature" or "every 5th attack spell is free".

A knight sees about 45 offers in 40 minutes (134 over three runs) and reaches level 16 to 18. Its whole pool is 54 steps, about 39 picks. By minute 40 its build is done.

Five structural problems

1. Hard walls everywhere

Imbuements stop at Powerful (room() returns 3 - tier), charms at III, grades at 3, run bonuses at 5 picks (RUN_BONUS_MAX), run luck at 50 (RUN_LUCK_MAX). The 6 imbuement slots (MAX_SLOTS) cap how many lines you have; the tiers cap how far each one goes. Once both are full, rollChoices has only fillers left.

2. Rarity is "more of the same", and often less than it says

  • An Epic Vampirism card from nothing skips Basic and Intricate and lands on 25% life leech in one click: the most exciting card is also the one that empties its line fastest.
  • An Epic charm takes you to tier III, which is +1% over tier II for every major charm (Wound 10% to 11%, Low Blow 8% to 9%). An Epic charm card is practically a Rare one.
  • Legendary (1%, x5) can't exist on imbuements, charms or grades. In the sim, 62% of Legendary rolls (57 of 92; the knight's worst, 11 of 14) were silently turned into something lower. The rarest event in the game mostly shows up as an Epic.

They just add up in damageMonster, so you can't see your real life leech anywhere. And there's a trap: Low Blow does nothing at all without Strike or Blood Rage (critChance = ... + (crit > 0 || bloodRage ? Low Blow : 0)). A player can pick Low Blow III and get zero.

4. Imbuement slots don't create choices

Six slots, up to 17 options for a knight, no structure. The sim paladin filled 4 of its 6 slots with protection imbuements (3 / 8 / 15% each) because they showed up. Nothing told it that its weapon had no leech or crit.

5. No class identity in the picks

Everyone gets the same 22 charms. A sorcerer is offered Overpower ("hit for 5% of your health") and Fatal Hold. Nothing in the pool says "knight": no extra melee swings, no casting for free.

3The vision in three rules

I. Lines, not tiers
Every pick (an imbuement, a charm, a spell grade, a run bonus, luck, a prey star) is a line with ranks. Ranks 1–3 are Tibia's real Basic / Intricate / Powerful values. Rank 4 and up follow one shared curve: each rank adds less, and none adds nothing.
II. Rarity adds layers
Every card is +1 rank. Rare adds a secondary stat. Epic also adds a vocation proc (a Wheel of Destiny perk). Legendary adds a relic instead of a proc. Same line, different card.
III. One sheet
All sources of a stat pool into one total with one cap in sight. The Upgrades window shows Life leech 31.4% (Vampirism 5, Vampiric Embrace 2, Front Sweep). Imbuements own stats, charms own procs, grades own spells. Overlap is visible, never hidden.

What changes for the player, in one paragraph

At level 60 the card screen still shows Powerful +4 Slash, a Rare Ethereal Spear grade that also gives 4% max health, and an Epic Vampirism with Combat Mastery, a 6% chance for melee hits to strike twice. All three are worth taking. Gold and potion cards are gone: a Skip button pays 75 gold per level, and potions are in the shop where they belong. Once or twice a run, an orange card shows up, and you shout.

4The curve: no walls

One formula for every line, so the game stays legible. Up to the knee K (Powerful = rank 3 for imbuements, charms and grades; 5 picks for run bonuses), values are exactly today's. Past the knee each rank adds a tail step s, divided by how far past the knee you are:

value(r) = value(K) + s · K · (Hr − HK)   where Hn = 1 + ½ + ⅓ + … + 1/n
Rank K+1 adds s · K/(K+1), rank 2K adds s/2, rank 3K adds s/3... It grows like a logarithm: it never stops, it just slows down. Percent-chance stats also get a rail, a safety limit set so high (life leech 50%, dodge 30%) that it takes about 20 ranks to get near it.

Skills and magic levels are whole numbers in Tibia's formulas, so skill lines floor their value (r10 = +10). Increments shrink but there's no wall: in the sim's 60-minute runs a heavily stacked line reaches rank 8 to 12, so nobody gets near a rail before the late game.

6Affix tables

Each line has an affinity: the secondaries and perks it can roll, filtered by vocation. A Rare Vampirism never rolls Focus (mana) for a knight; a Rare Void never rolls Battle Focus for a druid. Every secondary and perk is itself a line on the same curve, so getting the same one twice stacks.

7Legendary relics

A relic is a rule-breaker you can hold only once. A Legendary roll offers a relic you don't have, from the card line's family (weapon-flavoured lines roll weapon relics, healing lines roll the blessings). The vocation relics are named after real Tibia weapons, the universal ones after the real blessings. In the sim a 60-minute run rolls 5 to 10 Legendaries (charm cards included, and luck climbs to 50 fast). Today only 1 to 4 of them stay Legendary. With this pitch every one of them does, so relics need a limit of their own:

3 relic slots. Your first three Legendaries each add a new relic. After that, a Legendary awakens a relic you hold: relics have ranks too (Thunder Hammer chains to one more creature, Wand of Starstorm frees every 4th spell, then every 3rd...). Rule-breakers stay rare enough to remember, and Legendaries never go dead.

8Imbuements, charms, grades: a clean split

Imbuements = your gear
Flat stats: leech, crit damage, element share, skill and magic level, speed, protection. Limited by sockets on body slots, the way Tibia limits them by item. Ranks uncapped.
Charms = the creatures
Procs that react to creatures: on hit, on kill, when hit. Limited by 3 Major + 3 Minor charm slots (Tibia's own major/minor split, already in data.json). Ranks uncapped.
Grades = your spells
Per-spell bonuses from the Wheel. No slots: limited by the spells you know. Past grade 3, supreme ranks keep adding the gem's damage or healing.

Imbuement sockets (6, by body slot)

Socket#Takes (Tibia's item rules, simplified)

Armor1Vampirism, protections (Dragon Hide, Snake Skin, Quara Scale, Cloud Fabric, Demon Presence, Lich Shroud)

The decision is now real and readable: Strike or Vampirism on the second weapon socket? Only one protection fits on your armor unless you give up the shield socket. Sockets belong to the body slot, not the item, so buying a better helmet never wipes an imbuement.

A bestiary stage still offers 3 charm cards (rarity layers apply: a Rare Freeze also rolls Arcane Focus). Once your Major slots are full, Major cards only offer the charms you hold, at the next rank. Charm Expansion (a real Tibia store item) becomes a soul unlock: 4 + 4 slots.

The overlap, fixed

  • Pooled totals. Life leech, mana leech, crit chance and crit damage each become one number, Character.stat(key), summing every source (imbuement curve + charm curve + the casting spell's grade) and clamped by one rail. One place to read, one place to balance.
  • Low Blow works on its own. Any crit source makes your attacks crit-capable at 10%; Low Blow adds to that. No more dead picks.
  • Charm damage scales. Charm hits are capped at level × 2 today, so a level-80 Wound does at most 160 damage. The cap becomes level × 2 × (1 + 0.1 × ranks past 3): stacking a charm is worth it late.

11Why this over the others

AlternativeWhat it fixesWhere it falls short
Just add tiers 4 and 5Gives Legendary somewhere to landMoves the wall from minute 40 to minute 60. Rarity is still "more of the same". Overlap stays hidden.
A Wheel of Destiny talent treeDeep, very Tibia, class identityA tree is planned, not drafted: it kills the surprise of the card screen, which is the core of a survivors game. And a tree also ends when it's filled.
Vampire Survivors weapons (new auto-weapons per pick)Visual varietyFights the game's identity: Tibia's real spells and auto-attack are the weapons. Invented weapons would be off-tone.

Uncapped & UnequalKeeps every real Tibia value, keeps the draft, never runs dry, makes rarity a different card instead of a bigger one, gives each vocation its own perks and relics, and makes the overlap one readable number. The proof is cheap: the sim already counts filler share, and I've already instrumented it. The target is under 10% filler on offers 51–100, against 78–88% today, with no vocation's 60-minute power index beyond 1.5x the median.

12Risks and mitigations

RiskHow likelyMitigation
Runaway power: uncapped stacking plus relics snowball and a run never endsMediumHarmonic tail (rank 20 is worth about 6 ranks past the knee, not 17), rails on every chance stat, relics unique. The world already scales outward, so a strong build pushes deeper and meets harder monsters. Sim gate: no 60-minute run with a power index over 1.5x the median.
Card readability: up to four rows on a 232 px cardMediumOne row per layer, coloured by the rarity that adds it, with a short header. Common cards stay one row. Tooltips carry the "now → next" detail.
Tibia authenticity: ranks past Powerful, perks and relics are inventedCertain (by design)Ranks 1–3 are untouched real values. Every invented layer gets a Tibia name and goes in README's "Not real" list, as boosts and luck already do.
Pool dilution: more lines means less chance of the one you wantMediumSockets and charm slots cap how many lines are live. Banish removes a line for the run. Owned lines get offer weight x2 once sockets are full.
Relic balance: 24 rule-breakers, some will be duds or brokenHigh3 relic slots per run, extra Legendaries awaken a held relic. Ship 8 first (phase 3), the rest behind the Relic Codex, each with a sim A/B. Relics are data plus a small hook, so tuning is cheap.
Combat cost: more procs per hitLowPooled stats are cached and recomputed only on applyChoice. Perks are an array of the ones you hold, read in the hooks that already run.
Sim bot can't evaluate cards (it ranks by kind today)CertainAdd a scoring heuristic: marginal stat value per card from the curve, plus a flat bonus per perk or relic. Needed anyway for balancing.
Players miss the Epic "jump to Powerful"LowEpic now gives two ranks and a new behaviour. If tests say it isn't enough, Epic can give +2 ranks of the main line.
